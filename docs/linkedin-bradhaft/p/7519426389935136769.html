I spent a day inside the $159 BILLION company Stripe!!! Yes, I freaked out. No, I didn’t understand anything they said (jk i did and i made a video about it).

When I learned that Stripe’s engineering org was thousands of people big, I knew I wanted to figure out how so many people work together and build so freaking fast. Stripe ships hundreds of products, features, and improvements a year…and I wanted to see the internal systems helping them do all of it.

Adam Eagle, Staff Engineer at Stripe, let me follow him around for the day and meet the team building tools for Stripe’s own engineers.

We also got an inside look at Orbit, an internal AI hub where engineers can work through plans together, send Minions (not the yellow guys) off to write code, and keep several projects moving at once.

This was truly an incredible project to work on, and I’m so happy the team let Not My Business in!! There’s only so much you can understand about a company from the outside, and getting to sit in these rooms and ask my questions was very, very cool.

Huge thank you to Adam, Alicia, Michelle, Rami, and Molly, for letting me bother y’all and learn about what you’re building!!
