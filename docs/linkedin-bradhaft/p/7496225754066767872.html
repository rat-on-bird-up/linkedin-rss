EXPERIMENT OVER. Yesterday i got banned from LinkedIn.

this is my formal apology-after-being-canceled post.

which is funny, because for the last 3 months people have been asking me:

“why do you post such eye bleach things?”

“why do you change your bio every single day?”

“does your day job pay you to do this?”

simply, i was trying to break through the noise and make people laugh while doing it.

i run the branded social accounts for Clay, which means i spend all day thinking about how companies, creators, and execs should talk online.

the thing is, if you know me, i do not like talking like a professional. i do not have it in me.

this might handicap my corporate career, but truly, i do not want to be someone i'm not.

during my time in J-school and writing newsletters for 6AM City, and growing up reading Morning Brew, i learned to write/talk as if i was talking to a friend on the couch.

through watching wrastling, i learned to turn the dial 10% of who my “voice” is.

and through making LinkedIn my living, i studied the actual shapes of micro-blog-style posts.

together, this is my "sauce" and it can't be replicated.

and somehow, what started as a dumb competition with Owen Ciha to just rip more daily personal posts, mixed with jealousy over Sarah Khasrovi’s viral one-liners, mixed with watching Alex Lieberman turn an audience into power, brand deals, and distribuush, turned into 3,000 new followers in 3 months.

i changed my bio every day to match my weird posts and treated LinkedIn like a little lab for whatever was wrong with my brain that morning.

then yesterday i flew too close to the sun.

perhaps I used to many "💦" emojis.

but when my personal account got temporarily banned, it also put the brand accounts i run in a weird spot, because you need a personal account to manage company pages -- I nearly pooped myself.

SOOO. it’s safe to say the experiment worked, but idk if i’m returning to “the life” for a while. at least not in the current way.

truly became the most eye bleach account on here imo.

until next time.

✌️

(thanks LinkedIn for helping me)
